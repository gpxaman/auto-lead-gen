```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    CLIENT ARCHETYPE SPECTRUM                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
            │                                          │

│ • Core driver: Feasibility     │      │ • Core driver: Unit Margin    │
└───────────────────────────────┘      └──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Archetype A: The NPD Innovator / Deep-Tech Startup**
- Profile: Early-stage founders, patent holders, or funded hardware companies with an unrefined concept or non-manufacturable prototype.
- Primary Bottleneck: Bridging the gap from proof-of-concept (POC) to a production-ready engineering package.
- Strategic Angle: Sell de-risking and turnkey delivery. Lead with full DFM reviews, BOM cost reduction, and functional prototyping milestones.

**Archetype B: The E-Commerce Brand / Middleman / Sourcing Arbitrageur**
- Profile: Amazon/Shopify brands, wholesale resellers, and private-label distributors purchasing stock products from global trade directories.
- Primary Bottleneck: Product differentiation, high defect rates, fragile plastic enclosures, or excessive landed costs.
- Strategic Angle: Sell customization, cost-down engineering (VAVE), and quality assurance. Offer enclosure redesigns for proprietary branding, mold optimization for faster cycle times, and pre-shipment physical consignment inspection.

**Archetype C: The Overburdened SME / Mid-Market Engineering Team**
- Profile: Established industrial machinery, IoT, or consumer appliance companies experiencing internal engineering bandwidth constraints.
- Primary Bottleneck: Delayed project roadmaps; need specialized PCB routing, thermal analysis, or quick-turn DFM.
- Strategic Angle: Sell immediate execution and plug-and-play engineering bandwidth. Provide native format files and standard exchange formats (STEP, Gerber, 2D drawings) with zero onboarding friction.
