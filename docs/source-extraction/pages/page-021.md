To calculate the exact sales and profit under your hypothetical scenario, we have to look at the math with two massive constraints removed: labor cost and tooling cost.

You are describing a "Universal 3D Printer" that can print a 2-meter by 1-meter object (large enough for an engine block, a car panel, or a server rack) out of raw aluminum, while consuming only a few kilowatts of electricity, and printing everything down to integrated circuits.

If you possess this technology, you are no longer constrained by the traditional laws of manufacturing. You don't have to wait 6 weeks to cut a $20,000 injection mold, and you don't need a 20-person assembly line.

Here is what your actual daily profit and metrics would look like.

### 1. The Unit Economics of the "Universal Printer"

To be the most affordable in the market, we must look at your actual hard costs.

- Raw Material (Aluminum): In August 2026, global raw industrial aluminum trades at roughly $3.15 per kg (or about ₹345 per kg on the MCX).
- Electricity: A few kilowatts running for a few hours costs pennies per part.
- Labor & Tooling: $0 (The AI designs it, the universal printer creates it in one step).

If a client orders 100 custom laptop enclosures weighing 1 kg each, your hard cost to produce all 100 units is roughly $315 for the aluminum.

### 2. The Unbeatable Sales Strategy

In standard manufacturing, if a client wants 100 custom laptop enclosures, an agency like Xometry or Fictiv would charge them $8,000 for the CNC machining setup, plus $40 per unit, totaling $12,000.

With your technology, you can bid $2,500 for the whole job. You are nearly 80% cheaper than the global market, yet you are still marking up your $315 raw material cost by nearly 800%.

Because your AI bids instantly, offers free CAD/logo changes, and your price is a fraction of the competition, your conversion rate will skyrocket.

### 3. The Daily Metrics (The 2,000 Bid Scenario)

Assuming your autonomous AI system filters the 2 million daily global inquiries and bids on 2,000 highly qualified jobs per day:

- Bids Sent: 2,000 per day.
- Response Rate: 50% (1,000 clients). Because your price is 80% lower than standard factories and your delivery is instant, buyers will flock to your proposals.
- Close Rate: 80% of respondents (800 closed contracts per day). When a buyer sees a $2,500 quote for what should be a $12,000 job, with free revisions and top quality, they will sign immediately.
- Average Order Value: Let's assume a highly conservative average of $1,500 per order (since you are intentionally undercutting the market).

### 4. The Profit Calculation

- Daily Gross Revenue: 800 closed contracts × $1,500 = **$1,200,000 per day in top-line sales.**
- Daily Production Cost (Aluminum + Electricity): Because you are operating with near-zero labor and zero tooling, your gross margin on a $1,500 order is massive (roughly 80% to 90%). Let's assume an average cost of $250 per order for raw metal and shipping.
- Daily Gross Profit: 800 contracts × $1,250 profit per contract = **$1,000,000 per day in gross profit.**
